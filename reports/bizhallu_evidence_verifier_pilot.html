Protocol interpretation

What this prepares for the next detector comparison.

  • Internal uncertainty asks whether the model appeared uncertain while generating a token span.
  • A future evidence-aware verifier should independently decide whether a generated claim is supported by source evidence.
  • Confident wrong evidence binding is the key failure mode: a span can reuse real values but attach them to the wrong product, rank, or conclusion.
  • Among the presentation-labeled incorrect bindings, 7 were missed by all three displayed internal signals. The schema did not independently detect them.
